At a glance: Hourlio stores your workspace locally. No Hourlio account is required. The app does not automatically upload your business records to AKYN Labs. You choose when to export or share documents and backups.

1. About this policy

Hourlio — Time Tracker & Invoice is provided by AKYN Labs, the developer display name used for the application. This policy covers Hourlio for Android (package com.hourlio.hourlio) and explains browser storage where the web version is used. It also describes contact with AKYN Labs and visits to this policy page.

Privacy questions can be sent to akynlabs@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter and how it is used

Hourlio processes information you enter or import to provide its time tracking, project management, invoicing and reporting features. This may include:

  • Business Profile: business name, email, telephone, address, website, tax identifier, payment instructions and invoice prefix.
  • Clients and projects: client names, contact and billing details, project titles, descriptions, rates, currencies and status.
  • Time and invoices: timer sessions, time entries, notes, invoice numbers, dates, line items, tax amounts, totals, payment records and invoice history.
  • Preferences: currency, appearance and introduction-completion settings.

These details are used to maintain your workspace, calculate and display time and financial information, create invoice documents and support backup and restore. Hourlio does not process card payments or verify identities, transactions or the accuracy of invoice details. You are responsible for the client information you enter and for sharing it appropriately.

3. Local storage and access

On Android, business records are stored in a local SQLite database through Drift in the application's private storage. Preferences are stored locally through the platform preferences system. In the web version, records and preferences are stored in your browser profile for that site.

Hourlio does not operate a developer-hosted workspace database, require an account, or provide automatic cloud synchronization. The app has no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting integration and does not automatically transmit your workspace to AKYN Labs. AKYN Labs cannot remotely inspect or recover your local records through the app.

Local storage does not mean that no other copy can exist. Android or your device provider may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings and platform rules. Hourlio does not currently disable Android system backup explicitly. Browser, device-management and backup services have their own settings and privacy practices.

4. Invoice PDFs, downloads and sharing

Hourlio generates invoice PDFs locally using the invoice's business, client, work and payment details. Invoice records retain issuer and client snapshots, so changing the current Business Profile or a client's details does not necessarily change an existing invoice's stored information.

On Android, you choose a document destination for saving a PDF or an app through the system share sheet. On the web, supported browsers can share a PDF through the Web Share API; otherwise a clearly identified download option is available. Temporary local files may be created for previews and sharing; immediate deletion of every temporary copy is not guaranteed.

The app or service you select may receive, transmit and retain the document under its own privacy policy. Choosing a cloud folder, email app, messaging app or print service can send data off your device. Hourlio does not control those recipients or delete their copies. Opening sharing or downloading a PDF does not itself record payment or mark an invoice as sent.

5. JSON backup and restore

You can export an Hourlio JSON backup containing workspace records, including business and client information, projects, recorded time, invoices and supported preferences. The current backup format includes currency and introduction-completion settings; appearance settings remain local.

Backup files are plain, unencrypted JSON. Anyone with access to a file may be able to read its contents. The checksum helps detect accidental corruption; it does not encrypt the file or prove who created it. Choose a secure destination and control access to copies you save or share.

Backups are exported or shared at your request, not automatically uploaded by Hourlio. Restore reads a file you select, checks it, presents a review and requires confirmation before replacing the local workspace. Keep a separate usable backup before replacing records.

6. Retention, correction and deletion

Your workspace generally remains in local storage until you change it using the app, replace it by restoring a backup, clear the application's or website's data, or uninstall the app. Platform storage management can also remove local data. Some records are archived rather than erased; invoice snapshots and payment history may remain to preserve historical records.

You can review and correct current information through the app and export a backup. Clearing Android app storage or browser site data removes the local workspace; uninstalling normally removes the app's local data. These actions do not delete exported documents, backups, copies held by recipients or platform backups. Manage those copies separately with the relevant provider. Reinstallation or device transfer may restore data from a platform backup.

Hourlio has no online account to delete and AKYN Labs has no developer-hosted copy of your workspace to erase. Before deleting records, consider your applicable business and invoicing retention obligations. You can contact us about privacy questions or information you have sent directly to us.

7. Security and your choices

Hourlio relies on the operating system's application storage protections. It does not implement its own database encryption or encrypted backup format, and it makes no end-to-end encryption claim. Protect your device and browser profile, use appropriate device access controls and handle exported files carefully. No storage method or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

Android file operations use system document selection and sharing mechanisms. Selecting a file or recipient gives the relevant functionality access to that file; it does not grant AKYN Labs access to your whole workspace.

8. Contacting AKYN Labs

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and address the issue you raise. Email is handled through Gmail and any email provider you use, under their own privacy practices.

Please avoid sending invoice details, client information or a full backup unless necessary for your request. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of information you sent to us; applicable retention obligations may limit deletion. This is separate from data held only in your local Hourlio workspace.

9. Visiting this policy website

This page requires no login and contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, forms or embedded third-party content. It does not set its own cookies or use browser storage.

The hosting provider still receives technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website hosting does not give the host access to the Hourlio database stored on your device.

10. Children

Hourlio is designed for freelancers and business users and is not directed to children. AKYN Labs does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address the matter.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Hourlio's features, data handling or applicable requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to akynlabs@gmail.com.
